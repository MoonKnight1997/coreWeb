Simple & Fast Process

How It Works

Get comprehensive document verification in 4 easy steps – from upload to actionable recommendations

1

Upload Your Document

Upload your draft document in Word (.docx), PDF, or plain text format. Alternatively, paste your text directly into the verification form.

Supported formats: .docx, .doc, .pdf, .txt
Maximum size: 16MB
2

AI Analysis

Our AI agent analyzes your document against UK Employment Law standards, procedural rules (ET/EAT Rules 2024), and best practice guidelines.

Analysis includes:
  • Substantive law verification
  • Procedural compliance check
  • Pleading quality assessment
  • Case law verification
3

Receive Report

Get a detailed verification report with identified issues categorized by severity (Critical, Major, Minor) and actionable recommendations.

Report sections:
  • Executive summary
  • Issues by category
  • Recommendations
  • Risk assessment
4

Refine & Finalize

Use the verification report to improve your document. Address critical and major issues before filing. Seek qualified legal advice where needed.

Remember

This tool provides document analysis, not legal advice. Always consult a qualified solicitor or barrister for representation.

Typical Turnaround

Instant to 5 Minutes

Most documents are analyzed within 2-5 minutes. Complex documents with extensive case law may take slightly longer.

Supported Document Types

Comprehensive verification for all UK Employment Tribunal and EAT documents

ET1 / ET3 Forms

Employment Tribunal claim forms (ET1) and response forms (ET3) with verification of all mandatory sections and ACAS requirements.

Tribunal Submissions

Written submissions, witness statements, skeleton arguments, and other tribunal documents with quality and compliance checks.

EAT Appeals

Employment Appeal Tribunal grounds of appeal, skeleton arguments, and applications with permission requirements verification.

Applications

Amendment applications, reconsideration applications, and case management applications with proper basis verification.

Written Reasons

Responses to tribunal written reasons and requests for clarification with procedural compliance checks.

Other Documents

General employment law documents, legal submissions, and drafts requiring quality assurance and verification.

What This Tool Does NOT Do

Understanding the limitations ensures appropriate use of this verification tool
